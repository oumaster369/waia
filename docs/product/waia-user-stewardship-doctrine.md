# WAIA user stewardship, universal access and mutual support

**Status:** Proposed product doctrine. It becomes canonical only through Human Architect review and squash-merge of the DEE-612 pull request.

- **Owner:** Human Architect
- **Scope:** Product meaning and safe public-language boundaries
- **Implementation authority:** None by itself

## Purpose

WAIA is being built to serve people and to move toward a future in which the people who use it can steward it. It must not become a closed system whose objectives, incentives or use of human participation are hidden from those people.

This is a direction, not a statement about current legal ownership. Until an approved legal and economic structure exists, WAIA must not claim that users already own shares, equity, securities or governance rights.

## Preserve independent human perspectives

WAIA's value does not come from usage volume alone. Honest participation by more independent people can add lived experience, observations, corrections and outcomes to the shared picture from which people and their AI Twins learn.

Broader participation can make that picture richer and improve the possibility of coordination without erasing individuality. It does not guarantee completeness, objective truth or automatic consensus.

## Keep access independent of wealth

WAIA is intended to remain accessible to every living person regardless of economic position. Inability to pay should not, by principle, permanently exclude a person from WAIA.

Possible future mechanisms include a WAIA reserve or access fund, voluntary patron sponsorship and other transparent solidarity arrangements. These are directions only until separate approved implementation contracts establish funding, eligibility, duration, privacy, legal and sustainability rules.

Public copy may state the principle and direction. It must not promise permanent free access, guaranteed subsidy or an already-operational support mechanism.

## Practice mutual support without hierarchy

People with greater available resources may help preserve access for people with fewer resources. This support must not create superiority, dependency or control.

A patron or sponsor receives no default right to:

- control a supported person, their data or their AI Twin;
- gain additional governance or voting weight;
- determine another person's access conditions;
- expose or rank people who receive support.

Support status is private by default. Future reporting should use aggregate, non-stigmatizing measures unless a person gives explicit, informed consent to be named.

## Separate money from human weight

Financial participation is one kind of contribution to WAIA. Use, lived experience, observations, criticism, corrections and other meaningful participation also enrich the shared picture.

The amount of money a person contributes:

- records financial participation only;
- does not make that person's human contribution more valuable;
- does not create ownership, equity or a security;
- does not automatically grant governance power or voting weight.

## Permit truthful public language

### Directional copy

The following English copy is approved for product design only after this doctrine is Human-merged, and only while it remains clearly directional:

> **Built to belong to the people who use it**
>
> WAIA is being built toward a future in which it belongs to the people who use it — not to a closed corporation with interests hidden from them. The more independent people participate, the richer our shared picture of reality can become, and the better our chance of building tools that help people understand and coordinate with one another without losing their individuality.
>
> Access should not depend on wealth. Our intention is to develop transparent ways for a WAIA reserve fund or voluntary patrons to help preserve access for people who cannot afford it.
>
> Financial support sustains access. It does not make one human more important than another.

This copy describes intent. It must not be shortened in a way that turns future direction into a present legal or operational claim.

### Patrons copy boundary

DEE-611 may publish a minimal patrons surface backed only by the canonical DEE-617 public Treasury read model. It may use:

> People who help keep WAIA alive.

The public contribution list must display only consented public names, plus a private or anonymous aggregate when the server supplies one. Every contribution-share surface must include:

> Share shows financial participation only. It does not grant ownership, governance power or voting weight.

DEE-611 must not recompute canonical contribution totals in the frontend or imply that the reserve, sponsored access or Commons mechanisms are already operating.

## Gate future implementation

This doctrine authorizes no treasury transfer, subsidy, entitlement, custody or governance mechanism. Separate approved implementation issues are required for:

- reserve and access-fund ledger classification and reporting;
- sponsored-account or payment entitlements;
- eligibility, application, approval and duration rules;
- patron sponsorship flows;
- privacy, conflict and anti-abuse controls;
- auditable funding records without public beneficiary exposure;
- legal and jurisdictional review.

The conscious-contribution and fund-allocation economics remain owned by DEE-613. The Human-approved first rule protects one approved annual WAIA budget and accounts canonical free funds above it to the virtual Development Fund; the exact doctrine lives in [`waia-conscious-contribution-development-fund-doctrine.md`](waia-conscious-contribution-development-fund-doctrine.md). DEE-690 owns the separate backend implementation. Until that implementation truth is Human-merged, no product surface may claim that the Development Fund is operational. These mechanics must not be smuggled into the DEE-611 public Patrons page.
