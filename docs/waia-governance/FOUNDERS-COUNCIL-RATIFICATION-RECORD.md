## Purpose

This document is the official constitutional record that the Founders Council has ratified decisions **GI-01**, **GI-02**, and **GI-03** as defined in [`WAIA-GOVERNANCE-INTEGRATION-MASTER-PLAN-v1.0.md`](WAIA-GOVERNANCE-INTEGRATION-MASTER-PLAN-v1.0.md).

- **Relationship to the Master Plan:** The Master Plan is the **Phase 0 System of Record** (non-binding execution plan). This record provides the **decision evidence** for the Master Plan's Milestone M0 (Council Ratification) and the corresponding items on the Master Plan's Founders Council Ratification Checklist.
- **Relationship to Phase 0:** This record marks **Ratification Point 1** in the Master Plan PR sequence (§8). Its completion authorizes Phase 0 Governance Integration to proceed into PR1 preparation. It does **not** authorize Phase 1, any gate, any agent identity, or PR2.

This record does not restate or modify the substance of the decisions; the authoritative wording lives in the Master Plan. Where this record and any canonical governance document differ, the canonical document prevails.

## Ratified Decisions

### GI-01 — Governance Architecture & Authority Model

- **Decision summary:** Accept the consolidated governance architecture in which **WAIA Operations System v2.0** is the ecosystem-level constitution above the in-repo **WAIA DEV OS** (Development subsystem); the **Founders Council** is apex authority; and the **Human Architect** operates as the Council's delegate (Master Plan §3).
- **Scope:** Authority hierarchy, decision hierarchy, and reserved-decisions doctrine as recorded in the Master Plan. The operational-canon edits that make this hierarchy binding are performed later in **PR2 (GI-05)** — this ratification authorizes that sequence but does not itself rewire authority.
- **Approval statement:** The Founders Council approves GI-01 as defined in `WAIA-GOVERNANCE-INTEGRATION-MASTER-PLAN-v1.0.md` §3.

---

### GI-02 — English Canon Rule

- **Decision summary:** Adopt **English as the canonical language** for all governance, constitutional, operational, and architectural documentation. Translations are informational; on discrepancy, **English prevails** (Master Plan §5).
- **Carve-out summary:** The rule does **not** apply to verbatim product/UI copy and user-facing strings, nor to illustrative user-utterance examples in dialogue/architecture docs. These remain in their authored language as product data; an English gloss may accompany but must not replace the verbatim string.
- **Approval statement:** The Founders Council approves GI-02, including the carve-out, as defined in `WAIA-GOVERNANCE-INTEGRATION-MASTER-PLAN-v1.0.md` §5.

---

### GI-03 — Phase 0 Scope & PR Sequence

- **Decision summary:** Approve the **Additive-First** integration with a **two-PR primary sequence** — PR1 (Additive Constitutional Layer) then PR2 (Authority Reconciliation + Gate A cross-references), with PR3 as contingency only (Master Plan §8).
- **Explicit non-goals (Phase 0 does NOT attempt):** no agent identities or activation; no Gate B / Product Auditor; no Linear restructuring; no Discord, Voice of Humanity, Knowledge Base platform, Treasury, tokens, or WAIA Breathing; no code/runtime/schema/product change; no resolution of reserved decisions (Master Plan §2.2).
- **Approval statement:** The Founders Council approves GI-03 as defined in `WAIA-GOVERNANCE-INTEGRATION-MASTER-PLAN-v1.0.md` §2 and §8.

By signing below, the Founders Council confirms that:

- **no reserved decision is resolved** by this record (roadmap/capital allocation, tokenomics, portfolio, and all other reserved matters remain reserved);
