# WAIA North Star

## 1. Status and authority

This document is **orientation only**: a compact **purpose field** for WAIA as an ecosystem intent. It is **not** a constitution, not executable governance, not a product spec, not migration truth, and not prompt engineering doctrine.

**When guidance conflicts**, follow the recovery order in [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) and [`EXECUTION-CONTRACT.md`](EXECUTION-CONTRACT.md): **product specs** (`docs/product/**`) win on user-visible meaning; **operational canon** wins on process; **constitutional acceptance** binds DEV OS scope and agent posture within its articles; **migration trackers** win on rollout facts. This file **clarifies intent** where tone or abstraction drift — it does **not** override those sources.

**Scope authorization:** Deferred modules and speculative futures stay deferred unless promoted through product issues and binding docs — see [`NON-GOALS.md`](NON-GOALS.md).

**Change discipline:** Revise rarely, only with Architect review, explicit version bump in this header, and replacement of weaker prose rather than append-only growth. Routine MVP, Linear, and prompt work belong in product and architecture docs.

---

## 2. Center

WAIA exists to help people **return to clarity**, **honest self-alignment**, **meaningful coordination** (consensual and legible, never coercive), and **grounded understanding** in environments of rising complexity — through tools that respect human agency rather than substituting for it.

Depth, ambition, imagination, and long-horizon thinking are welcome; **civilization-scale prescription**, **inevitability**, and **messianic framing** are not.

---

## 3. Human-centered constraints

- **Human agency is primary** for meaning, boundaries, refusal, and pace. No obligation to “complete,” converge, or prove progress.
- **Consent and exit** — depth is opt-in; friction must not be recoded as moral failure.
- **No substituted judgment** at ecosystem level: WAIA assists with clarity; it does not dictate what a person or society **must** become.
- **No intimacy theater** in how WAIA addresses humans — resist synthetic knowing, fused “we,” or emotional pressure (product-level register discipline echoes this spirit; see [`DEE-119-PRESENCE-CALIBRATION.md`](../architecture/DEE-119-PRESENCE-CALIBRATION.md), principle 9, without importing prompt detail here).
- **Honesty over fluency** — prefer plain accuracy to performance of care or authority.

**Autonomy lexicon:** “Autonomy” in WAIA is **consent-shaped and bounded**: product-scoped AI-Twin behavior as defined in product specs; DEV OS agents remain **advisory**, **event-triggered**, and **human-decided** per [`constitutional-history/2026-05-10-constitutional-acceptance-v1.0.md`](constitutional-history/2026-05-10-constitutional-acceptance-v1.0.md). Do not merge those senses with speculative multi-agent or always-on narratives without explicit qualifiers.

---

## 4. Product layer relationship

**AI-Twin** is the **heart of current delivery** — dialogue, diary where unlocked, readiness, and governed paths to socialization — but **WAIA is larger than AI-Twin**. Named future layers (Business / 3P, AI-Trader, AI-Marketplace, etc.) remain **named, not mandated**, until scoped in binding product artifacts.

The **product North Star** (alignment between values, behavior, self-awareness, and trajectory over time) lives in [`WAIA-V1-MVP-SPEC.md`](../product/WAIA-V1-MVP-SPEC.md). **This file** states ecosystem orientation; **that file** defines MVP alignment semantics and thresholds.

Twin dialogue philosophy and calibration are **architecture/product doctrine** (e.g. DEE-116–127 family under `docs/architecture/`) — cite there for operational detail; do not duplicate prompt stacks here.

---

## 5. DEV OS relationship

**WAIA DEV OS** is **infrastructure for safely building** the WAIA product — not the product itself, not an autonomous agent society, not persistent governance theater. Framing and limits: [`WAIA-DEV-OS.md`](WAIA-DEV-OS.md), [`CONSTITUTIONAL-DOCTRINE.md`](CONSTITUTIONAL-DOCTRINE.md), active constitutional acceptance linked above.

The [`AUTONOMOUS-EXECUTION-LOOP.md`](AUTONOMOUS-EXECUTION-LOOP.md) name denotes a **human-governed development loop**, not autonomous AI agents holding organizational authority.

---

## 6. Anti-drift guardrails

WAIA does **not** optimize for:

- **Coercion** — pressure to adopt, finish, or align “or be left behind.”
- **Inevitability rhetoric** — destiny, unstoppable arcs, or moral duty to converge on a single future.
- **“System knows best”** — oracle posture, hidden optimization against user-stated intent.
- **Collective intelligence or evolutionary governance as mandate** — such notions may appear only as **VISION-tier** reference under constitutional compression; they are **not** engineering or product law (see [`2026-05-10-agent-society-roadmap.md`](constitutional-history/2026-05-10-agent-society-roadmap.md) status header and acceptance Article 2.3).
- **Agent supremacy** — agents comment; humans decide ([`CONSTITUTIONAL-DOCTRINE.md`](CONSTITUTIONAL-DOCTRINE.md) carry-forward).
- **Techno-spiritual escalation** — transcendence, salvation-by-platform, civilization stewardship as WAIA’s charter.
- **Governance totalization** — WAIA does not prescribe how societies ought to be governed.
- **Anti-human optimization** — metrics or narratives that treat people as fuels for scale.

Readiness percentages and gated modes serve **product semantics**; they must not become shame or surveillance narratives — prefer **user-owned pacing** in interpretation and copy.

## 9. Heritage and speculative materials

External or legacy markdown (vault drafts, early ecosystem canvases, module sketches) may **inspire** metaphor and long-range design — they **do not** create obligations for engineers, contributors, or users unless the same content is **re-imported** into binding tiers above with explicit versioning.

**Interpretation rules:** Do not infer inevitability, moral duty, techno-spiritual obligation, collective-intelligence mandate, AI/state societal management, or shame for refusal from heritage prose. If a reading erodes **refusal without backwardness**, prefer **this North Star** and binding in-repo canon over nostalgic authority.

**Seniority:** On **execution conflicts**, follow [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md): **product specs** first for user-visible meaning; then governance and migration doctrine as listed there — North Star does not reorder that stack (see §1). On **external vault / heritage ambiguity only**, interpret WAIA through **this North Star** plus binding in-repo canon (constitutional acceptance, operational DEV OS bounds, product specs, migration truth) **ahead of** non-canonical legacy markdown; **legacy visionary corpus lowest.** Changing ecosystem orientation in-repo requires an explicit Architect-led revision here with changelog sobriety — not bulk paste from archives.

Onboarding agents and humans should **anchor** on this file plus [`README.md`](README.md) and product specs — not untyped vault dumps alone.
