## First supported method and actual issuance

### Strict anchor and finite signed-delta coverage

The first account-basis method is a versioned strict-anchor, finite signed-delta method. It requires an independently justified, source-dated Reality anchor for the entire enrolled spot account, plus an explicit finite cumulative positive/negative quantity bound for every relevant asset and mutation channel from the anchor's valid time through the basis expiry. It does not create a new Reality time representation or an interval-only positive bootstrap.

A typed immutable source profile must name:

- Exact organization/account/venue/market, credential/assignment identities, method/schema version, actual provider-contract/evidence records and their retained provenance.
- The complete asset/liability universe and supported account classes. Ordinary and conditional orders, fills, transfers/deposits/withdrawals, fees and every other possible enrolled external/manual/other-key effect are either explicitly covered or UNBOUNDED/unsupported. Absence from an observed page does not exclude a channel. Unsupported borrowing/liability or unknown assets refuse this first spot method.
- The source's asserted-time semantics, explicit maximum age/skew and validity rules, finite pagination/member/work limits, and the exact evidence making a bound enforceable or observable. Acquisition timeout is an engineering bound, not a freshness guarantee.
- Per-channel, per-asset nonnegative cumulative positive and negative quantity bounds over a named full interval, with exact source-contract provenance and inclusion/overlap identities. No rate is extrapolated beyond its actually admitted interval. No configured trading cap or envelope is used as a bound on external activity.
- The governing envelope/allocation/allowed-market references, qualified temporal-reference method and complete member schedule, and explicit expiry/revocation conditions. Missing values have no permissive defaults.

For each covered asset, the strict anchor supplies canonical quantity Q. Compute the conservative upper quantity from Q plus all permitted positive changes; compute the guaranteed lower quantity from Q minus all permitted negative changes, floored at zero. Treat pending/uncertain inclusion conservatively: possible sells never reduce the upper exposure, possible buys never increase guaranteed inventory, and a possibly already included effect remains charged until exact independent inclusion is established. Do not net unrelated uncertain changes favorably. Convert quantities/debts using the same pinned canonical exposure unit and qualified reference identity. This is a bound on account quantities and charges under the admitted source/mutation model, not a guarantee about unknowable future market prices.

The basis expiry is no later than the intersection of actually supported anchor/source/profile/reference/envelope windows. Charge uncertainty over that entire window when publishing, not only through publication time. At each later admission the finite bound remains valid only while all its exact governing identities and windows hold. If any channel is unbounded, required source disappears, currentness is unknown or an independent anchor is missing, retain evidence and refuse; L6 source-loss HALT is not relaxed because an old numeric interval is finite.

Unknown-time acquired members retain `UNKNOWN` and the actual acquisition/knowledge interval in the new observational record. They cannot populate existing strict Reality `validAtUtc`, cannot replace a missing initial anchor, and cannot become RECONCILED by a cast. No HTTP Date, request-completion time, order-created time or sequence number is silently substituted for absent state-valid time. The existing HTX adapter's caller-supplied `RawHtxObservationContextV2.validAtUtc` is a type requirement, not independent proof of that time.
